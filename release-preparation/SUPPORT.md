# Support Guide

Need help with TaintFlow? Here are the best ways to get support from the maintainers and community.

---

## Technical Questions & Discussion
- **GitHub Discussions**: For general questions, setup support, custom rules development, and feature requests.
- **Discord Community**: Join our developer Discord server to chat with other TaintFlow users and core maintainers.

---

## Filing Issues
If you encounter a bug, scanner crash, or incorrect scan metric:
1. **GitHub Issues**: File a report using the appropriate issue template.
2. **Minimal Working Example (MWE)**: Always provide a small snippet of code that reproduces the problem.

---

## Response Times (SLA)
While TaintFlow is primarily a community-driven open-source project, our core maintainers aim to respond within:
- **Security vulnerabilities**: Acknowledged within **24 hours** (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
- **GitHub Issues**: Reviewed and triaged within **3 business days**.
- **Pull Requests**: Reviewed within **5 business days**.
